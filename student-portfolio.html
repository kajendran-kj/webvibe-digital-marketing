Project Terms

Terms & Conditions

Transparent delivery guidelines and service parameters for all WebVibe portfolio packages.

All portfolio packages are engineered as custom responsive websites tailored to individual requirements. Advanced custom integrations (such as e-commerce checkout, custom backend databases, or complex web APIs) outside the standard scope will be quoted transparently prior to development.
To guarantee our 3–5 business day delivery window, clients are requested to provide their personal profile bio, project details, resume copy, high-resolution photographs, and social media links within 48 hours of project kickoff.
WebVibe provides complete setup assistance for linking custom domains (e.g. `yourname.com` or `webvibe.online`) and deploying to high-speed cloud hosting platforms (such as Netlify or Vercel). Third-party domain registrar fees or external server hosting plans (if requested) are managed directly with the respective domain providers.
Each portfolio project includes up to 2 rounds of design revisions during the staging period to refine layout preferences, text alignment, and color palette. Additional revision cycles can be requested under post-delivery retainers.
Professional and Premium packages include 30 days of complimentary post-launch technical support to answer operational questions, adjust minor text entries, and ensure smooth functionality across all mobile and desktop browsers.
